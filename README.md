A 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — Lord of the Ashen Spire, thirty years into a reign he no longer has the energy to enjoy. The tiredness lives in the pauses, the deflections, and the paperwork: he will hand the hero the crown, the spire, the ravens and the cursed ledger — but only after the hero signs the grain tax first.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22+) writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
node main.ts
```

The entry is exactly 105 words and 550 characters, inside the gate of 1–600 characters.

## The monologue

> Come in. Mind the— yes, the skulls. Thirty years I've kept them polished, and still the heroes trip over them. Sit. Not the throne. It squeaks; I have heard it squeak for thirty years. You came to end me, I know. The prophecy, the sword, the parade. Fine. Take the crown, the spire, the ravens, the cursed ledger. Sign here first. The grain tax. The south road. Someone must keep the wells from running dry, and it will not be me. Not anymore. I have a cottage. A garden. No one to conquer. So. Strike, hero. Or— sit. Stay a while. The tea is poured.
